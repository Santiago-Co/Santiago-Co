## Robotics: 4Space Uniovi

35-student University of Oviedo team that designs and builds a Mars rover for the [European Rover Challenge](https://roverchallenge.eu/team/4space-uniovi-2026/). Team member since February 2026. At the European Rover Challenge 2026, the team improved its result by 75% over the 2025 edition.

**GNC Subsystem Lead** · September 2026 – present

In September 2026 I took over the navigation subsystem, merging control and navigation into a single GNC (Guidance, Navigation and Control) subsystem.

- Lead a 4-person team: task planning, code reviews, technical documentation and onboarding of new members.
- Develop ROS 2 nodes on Linux for the rover's software.
- Program the rover's ESP32-S3 microcontrollers, networked and powered over Ethernet (PoE).
- In progress: 3D LiDAR integration, autonomous navigation and a rosbag analysis tool to review each mission.

**Rover GUI and Ground Station Lead** · May 2026 – September 2026

- Led the rover's GUI and ground station (GS): frontend and backend to operate and monitor the rover.

_The team's code is private, so it is not published on this profile._

`Java 17` · `Maven` · `Swing (MVC)` · `SQLite` · `Jackson` · `JUnit`

Desktop application for managing urban incidents with four roles (citizen, operator, technician and lead technician). Built by a team of three (Jose Angel Prada Zamora, Mario Acuña and me) for the Information Systems course at the University of Oviedo.
